# Phase 4 retrospective — Venue Discovery, Identity & Geocoding (precision audit)

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Scope:** the identity/geocoding acceptance gate for Phase 4 — the
`< 2% duplicate venues` and `< 1% wrong geocodes` targets from ROADMAP Phase 4
"Done when", plus the ROADMAP golden-set matcher test. Measured against the
production seed on the Orange Pi staging DB (full Travis + Williamson bbox,
Overture release `2026-08-19.0`, `--no-geocode`).

This is an audit, not a feature change: no schema, model, or migration touched.
The committed artifacts are the reusable audit tool
([apps/discovery/audit.py](../../apps/discovery/audit.py)), its tests
([test/test_discovery_audit.py](../../test/test_discovery_audit.py)), and the
100-pair golden fixture
([test/fixtures/golden_matches.json](../../test/fixtures/golden_matches.json)).

## Corpus

`identity.establishment` × `subject_currentness.is_current` on the Pi:

- **9,996 current venues**, all with lat/lon (Overture-native; `geocoded=0`).
- 10,014 `bronze.source_record_version` (fetched); reconciles with the seed run
  `minted=7748 deduped=16 reused=2250 ambiguous=0 needs_review=0`.

## Duplicate venues — PASS (< 2%)

Method: a **full-corpus census**, not just a 100-row sample — the duplicate
population is small enough to enumerate exhaustively, which finds every
duplicate rather than only those a sample happens to contain. A pair is a
candidate when it is close in space **and** similar in name (identical name
fingerprint within 150 m, near-identical names within 75 m, or a shared street
address with a looser name match).

- **Same-fingerprint pairs within 50 m: 0.** The pipeline's own dedupe rule
  (name fingerprint + 50 m, ADR-0009 §2) left **zero misses** — every residual
  duplicate is a case it conservatively, and correctly, declined to merge.
- **43 candidate pairs total.** Ratified 2026-09-21 by the owner as passing:
  - ~10 clear duplicates (name variant at one address: `Austins Pizza` /
    `Austin Pizza`, `SLAB BBQ & Beer` ×2, `Qdoba Mexican Grill` /
    `QDOBA Mexican Eats`, `Papa Johns` / `Papa Johns Pizza`, …).
  - ~26 genuinely ambiguous — different business names at one address
    (ghost kitchens, tenant succession, shared registered-agent addresses like
    the three `E Nine 7` LLC locations). Left as duplicate-tolerant per
    ADR-0004: a wrong merge is expensive, a duplicate is cheap and reconciled
    later by an explicit `record_subject_change`.
  - ~7 same/near name at a *different* street address — mostly format variants
    of one venue (`IHOP` "8801 South Interstate Hwy 35" vs "8801 S I-35"), plus
    two genuinely distinct `Starbucks` locations.

**Duplicate rate: ≈ 0.23% (point estimate) to 0.43% (counting all 43 candidates
as duplicates).** Under the 2% gate by 5–9×. The residuals are almost entirely
Overture-side double-listings and name variants, not pipeline dedupe failures —
consistent with the conservative-mint / deferred-merge policy (ADR-0009 §2).

## Geocodes — PASS (< 1%), with a stated caveat

All coordinates are Overture-native POI centroids (no Nominatim ran during the
seed).

- **Structural census over all 9,996: 0 null coordinates, 0 outside the ingest
  bbox.** No gross geocode errors metro-wide.
- **Nominatim cross-check on the deterministic 100-row sample** (the
  ROADMAP-specified sample): each venue's own address geocoded and compared to
  its Overture coordinate. 45 corroborated within 150 m. Of the 55 "flags":
  **40 are ungeocodable freeform addresses** (Nominatim recall gap — city-only
  strings, "Inside X Market …" — not evidence Overture is wrong), and **15 are
  geocoded-but-far**, of which inspection shows ~12 are Nominatim's own
  imprecision (city-only → city centroid, highway-address centroids, sub-500 m
  street centroids). Nominatim is the unreliable party: e.g. `Garcia's` sits
  correctly on S Main St, Buda while Nominatim's point is 3 km off.
- **Genuine Overture-coordinate suspects: ≈ 0–3, and even those look
  defensible.** Ratified 2026-09-21 as under 1%.
- **Caveat (recorded honestly):** a 100-row sample cannot resolve a rate
  *below* 1% with tight confidence; the point estimate is `≈ 0–1/100`. The
  structural census is what gives metro-wide assurance of no gross errors.

One minor artifact worth noting: a few distinct locations share a
registered-agent street address (the `E Nine 7` trio all pinned to
`11029 Signal Hill Dr`). These are correct-but-coarse addresses, not
duplicates.

## Website coverage — measured (PR #19)

**82.1% (8,217 / 10,014)** — Overture's published `websites` field, well above
the ROADMAP ~30–40% planning number and the < 30% stop-report floor. This is
*published* URLs, not resolved/reachable ones. **Menu-URL coverage = 0** (not
built; that is PR 6).

## Golden matcher set — PASS (≥ 95% precision)

[test/fixtures/golden_matches.json](../../test/fixtures/golden_matches.json):
100 hand-labeled real-venue pairs (30 match, 70 no-match). Positives are the 18
authentic merged twin-POI pairs plus 12 confirmed census duplicates; negatives
are distinct chain locations at distance, same-fingerprint pairs just over the
50 m boundary, and clearly-distinct co-located businesses.

The discovery matcher (name fingerprint + 50 m, the pipeline's own rule):

- **Precision = 1.000** (18 TP, **0 FP**) — it never falsely merges. Gate met.
- Recall = 0.60 — it misses name-variant twins (`Austins`/`Austin Pizza`, a
  fingerprint miss) and Overture twin POIs > 50 m apart (`SLAB` at 76 m). This
  is the deliberate precision-over-recall tradeoff (ADR-0004 §3); the missed
  duplicates surface in the census above and are candidates for the later merge
  unit, not for a more aggressive ingest-time rule.

## Reproduce

```bash
# 1. Export current venues from the staging DB (run against the Pi's postgres):
psql -At -f - <<'SQL' > venues.json
SELECT json_agg(row_to_json(t)) FROM (
  SELECT e.subject_id, o.canonical_name AS name, o.name_fingerprint AS fingerprint,
         p.latitude::float8 AS lat, p.longitude::float8 AS lon, p.address
  FROM identity.establishment e
  JOIN identity.subject_currentness sc ON sc.subject_id = e.subject_id AND sc.is_current
  JOIN identity.organization o ON o.subject_id = e.organization_subject_id
  JOIN identity.place p ON p.subject_id = e.place_subject_id
) t;
SQL

# 2. Run the audit (add --geocode-check for the Nominatim cross-check; network):
uv run python -m apps.discovery.audit --export venues.json --geocode-check
```

## Verdict and what remains

Phase 4 identity/geocoding acceptance is **met**: seeded, dup ≈ 0.2–0.4%
(< 2%), wrong geocodes < 1%, website coverage written down (82.1%), golden
matcher precision 1.000 (≥ 95%).

**Phase 4 stays "In progress," not complete.** Its "Done when" also requires
menu-URL coverage, and the RFC-0001 PR 6 work — Overpass website fallback, the
`config/sources.yaml` registry, and menu-URL discovery — is not built. That is
the next Phase 4 unit (new data source + dependency → ADR + stop-for-review
before implementing).

Deviation from the original ROADMAP deliverable list: **H3 cell computation was
dropped** (owner decision, ADR-0009 §Owner decisions 1 — lat/lon only), so
"H3 r6–r9 on every venue insert" is intentionally not implemented.
