**Author(s):**

RFCs are for proposals bigger than a single ADR-sized decision — new
subsystems, cross-cutting changes, or anything where the *shape* of the
solution is still open. If the decision is narrow and the options are
already clear, write an ADR instead.

## Summary

One paragraph: what is being proposed and why.

## Motivation

What's broken, missing, or about to become a problem if we don't act?
Who is affected?

## Detailed design

The actual proposal. Enough detail that someone could implement it, or
poke real holes in it. Include interfaces, schema sketches, data flow —
whatever the proposal actually touches.

## Drawbacks

Why might we *not* want to do this?

## Alternatives

What else was considered, and why this approach over those.

## Unresolved questions

What isn't settled yet, and who needs to weigh in before it can be.
