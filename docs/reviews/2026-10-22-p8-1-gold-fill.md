# Gold fill on helios_laptop: the first real price index (ADR-0019 build step 1)

Session P8-1, 2026-10-22. This carries out [ADR-0019](../adr/0019-public-v1-site.md) §5 build
step 1 under the owner's Q6c approval (2026-10-21). After P5-33's laptop step (the ADR-0015
re-verification and the R4 rescore, [ADR-0013 Amendment 27](../adr/0013-phase5-menu-pipeline.md)
build note, PR #102), it takes a fresh dump of `helios_laptop`, runs `python -m apps.gold.refresh`
there, and measures what Gold now holds.

Scope:
- the only `helios_laptop` writes were the Gold refresh, which writes only the `gold` schema, and
  the restore check, which wrote a scratch `*_test` database that was then dropped (the dump
  itself only reads). P5-33 ran its own writes; this session ran none of them;
- every other read was a plain SELECT in a `default_transaction_read_only` session. That includes
  the API, which ran locally on 127.0.0.1 with `PGOPTIONS=-c default_transaction_read_only=on`;
- no schema, migration, model, dependency, `infra/` or Pi change. `helios_laptop` stays at alembic
  `db40e9424cba`, one revision behind `main` (section 6, Q1).

**Result: Gold has rows, and the area index is even thinner than F3 projected: 97.3% of 0.01°
cells are `low_sample` (F3: 95.9%). 900 venues serve a menu through the API, three times the
300-venue milestone.** The whole delta from F3 is accounted for, family by family. R4 removed 22
families. A further 58, which F3's proxy counted, have priced rows only in the `Unsectioned`
grouping, which never reaches Gold (section 5.2). Every one of the 9,286 served sections has
`name: null`, as F2 predicted. That is ADR-0019 step 2, now proposed as ADR-0006 Amendment 1.

### 5.2 The delta, explained

The proxy (ADR-0019 appendix) was re-run on today's Silver in three forms, each read-only
(`var/p8-1/measure.sql`, `delta.sql`):

| Step | Families | Shared | Placed families | 0.01° cells | Low | Share low | Venues in ≥ 5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| F3 proxy, verbatim (it still counts tombstoned pages) | 981 | 505 | 476 | 267 | 256 | 95.9% | 93 |
| … with R4 applied (pages under a `withdrawal` head left out) | 959 | 495 | 464 | 263 | 253 | 96.2% | 88 |
| … and only families with a sectioned priced row = **Gold** | **901** | **448** | **453** | **259** | **252** | **97.3%** | **72** |

- **R4 (−22 families).** The 22 withdrawn families that had prices (section 3). Their 12 placed
  families were mostly in thin cells: 4 cells and 3 low cells went, and the share rose 0.3 points.
- **`Unsectioned`-only families (−58).** These families' priced rows (1,405 of them) all sit in
  the structural `Unsectioned` grouping. `enumerate_current_requests` finds no native path for
  them, so they never enter `current_menu` (ADR-0007 Amendment 1 §7). F3 listed what its proxy
  leaves out, but not this. It removed 11 placed families: 4 more cells (all below 5) went,
  and 3 cells fell below 5, so ok cells went from 10 to 7, and venues in them from 88 to 72.
  - Wider than these 58 families: 8,603 of the 66,677 priced rows on current heads (12.9%) are
    `Unsectioned`. 1,405 of them are these 58 families' rows; the rest sit in families that
    also have sectioned rows. Gold serves none of them (Q4).
- **Shared menus fall with the families (505 → 448).** Most of the 58 `Unsectioned`-only
  families are chain locations, so the shared count drops by 47 of the 58.
- **Two smaller differences in kind, not in number.** The proxy reads the capture endpoint's
  `canonical_uri`, while the refresh reads the price Evidence's endpoint
  (`source_endpoint_for_evidence`). The proxy also counts a family's rows directly, while Gold
  keeps one row per target and context (52,651 rows against 58,074 sectioned priced
  observations; this collapse was not decomposed). Neither moves a family or a cell here: the
  third row matches Gold exactly.
