## 2. The add-on rule (owner Q4)

Rule, from the owner's ruling:
- An add-on is an entry the page frames as an addition to, or a change of, another item, not
  ordered on its own. Examples:
  - "Add 2 Eggs $2.99", "+ avocado $2", "extra dressing", "sub fries +$2", "make it a combo";
  - an entry under an Add-ons, Extras, Toppings, Add-ins, Substitutions or "Add protein"
    heading;
  - a side listed in an add-on list ("Papas $2.95");
  - a surcharge (gluten-free crust +$4).
- It is not an item, so it leaves `items` and recall's denominator. It is kept in the label's
  `addons` list.
  - `attaches_to` names the item it modifies when the page's order makes that clear: the
    add-on line printed under that one dish, before the next.
  - Otherwise it is `null`: a section-wide add-on list or a separate Add-ons section.
- Sides in a Sides section, drinks, combos with "+" in the name, a "Subs" sandwich section
  and build-your-own base items stay items.

Method:
- **Candidates** (`var/p8-16/addon_candidates.py`): every label item in the 873 reviewed menu
  labels of the dev selection (the 793 scored pages and 80 without saved answers) that the
  wording marks. The marks are its name, its printed line, its section, the nearest heading
  above it, or a "+$" price. That gives 1,078 candidates on 138 pages, deliberately broad.
- **Review:** four sonnet subagents applied the rubric
  (`var/p8-16/addon-review/rubric.md`) to page packets. A packet shows each candidate with
  the printed blocks around it and the label's neighbouring items. The verdicts were 535
  add-on, 412 item and 131 borderline.
- **Re-check (this session):**
  - All 535 add-on verdicts were read against their printed evidence. Four were moved to
    borderline: `fa377c80652b`'s "add chicken | 16.5" and its siblings. These print the dish's
    full price with chicken, while the page prints surcharges as "+3", so they read as
    variants.
  - The item verdicts were scanned for add-on wording and add-on sections; none changed.
- **Result:** 531 add-ons on 90 pages (493 on P8-13's pages, 38 on the P8-14 set), 567 gold
  prices. The appendix's section 3 lists each one, with its section, what it attaches to and
  the printed words that decide it.
