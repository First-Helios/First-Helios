# The PDF reader: `pdf-v1`, the `menu_pdf_only` fetch branch and validator-v13 (P8-23, ADR-0029 D2–D5)

Session P8-23, 2026-11-19. Built on the laptop while the Orange Pi ran the long recrawl, under
the owner's delegation for the decisions within the plan ("I approve every data write below and
delegate the decisions within this plan to you"). Stacked on #164 (ADR-0029, the `pypdf`
dependency), which isn't merged: **no PDF data reaches the public database until the owner
merges #164 and this PR.** Tested on a scratch `*_test` database only; `helios_laptop` and the Pi
are not written by this branch.

## Result

- **`pdf-v1`** (`packages/helios_parsing/pdf.py`): `pypdf` plain extraction of at most 40 pages;
  each non-empty line is one `<p>`, pages split by `<hr>`; an encrypted or unopenable file is
  `pdf_unreadable`, one with under 200 characters of text `pdf_no_text`. The document goes
  through the existing segmenter (`segment-v2`), so everything after the reader is unchanged.
- **Discovery** (`menu_pdf_only` branch): when no page verifies and a fetched page links a
  menu-named PDF, that first PDF is fetched (a PDF link may now redirect to another host if it
  ends in a PDF there; robots.txt per hop, public addresses and the 3 MB cap kept), read and
  judged by the production page check on the derived HTML. Accepted: the PDF link is the menu
  URL (signal `pdf`). Otherwise the site's Capture says why: `menu_pdf_only` (unfetched, or
  rejected by the page check), `not_pdf`, `pdf_unreadable`, `pdf_no_text`. A saved PDF menu is
  re-verified by a fresh read; the three new reasons withdraw it like `not_html`.
- **Menu-page fetch** (`page_bronze`): a PDF response is read into a Version (payload
  `reader: pdf-v1`; HTML payloads unchanged, so no HTML page gets a new Version); the bundle keeps
  the PDF's bytes as `body` and the document as `rendered_dom`, which `page_html` already returns
  to the page check, the extractor and [5c]/[5d]. A `.pdf` link that answers with anything else
  is `failed/not_pdf`. Before this branch every PDF response was skipped (`pdf`).
- **validator-v13** (D5): a block of at least 6 number-only tokens that are at least two thirds
  of its tokens is a numeric table's row: no bare reading, no `bare_page` vote; its money still
  reads. **0 of 2,504 labelled or published texts change; 2 of 2,010 stored texts change, and
  Gold loses at most one row per venue on one page** (section 3).

## 1. Build decisions (decided under the owner's delegation)

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| Evidence locators keep `segment-v2` (the ADR's D3 text says `blocks:pdf-v1:…`) | `segment-v2` makes the blocks (from pdf-v1's document), and `excerpt` checks a locator's segmenter against the bundle's. `pdf-v1` is recorded in the Version payload (`reader`) and is implied by the bundle (`application/pdf` body + derived `rendered_dom`). |
| The PDF link, not the redirect target, is the menu URL | A file CDN's target can be signed or moved; the link is what the venue's site prints, and its key is an own-site key like an HTML page's. |
| A PDF link that leaves its site must end in a PDF | Otherwise a `.pdf` link redirecting to an HTML page on another host could be verified as the venue's menu (discovery also crawls `.pdf` links as candidates). |
| An encrypted PDF is `pdf_unreadable` (no empty-password attempt) | The ADR's text; 0 of M2's files are encrypted, so nothing is lost today. |
| D5 counts rows "at least two thirds number-only", not "numbers only" | Olive Garden's rows print "less than 1 g" in some cells: numbers only left 25 bare readings, two thirds leaves 0; "Party Pack 12 tacos 2 queso 1 rice 1 beans 1 salsa 45" still reads 45. |
| validator-v13 is recorded in ADR-0029 D5 and this review, not as an ADR-0013 amendment | ADR-0013 on main has no section for Amendment 36 (validator-v12) either (doc drift, recorded in the P8-23 run-record PR); keeping v13 here avoids two PRs appending to ADR-0013. |
| `menu_pdf_only` keeps its meaning (PDF linked, not taken); new counters `menu_pdf_read`, `menu_pdf_unread` | Reports stay comparable with the 164 venues counted before. |

One site changes: **Cap City Comedy** now captures `CAP CITY FOOD MENU.pdf` (on the club's S3
bucket, linked from its showroom page). It is the club's food menu (verifier 1.0 / 1.0), but the
fixture was labelled before PDFs were read and expects an image menu on the showroom page, so the

**A reader change found here:** plain extraction printed that PDF one word per line (545 blocks
of one word). `pdf-v1` therefore reads a page in layout mode when plain mode leaves at least 30
non-empty lines of which at least 80% are single words (70 clean blocks for Cap City). None of
M2's files trips it: their documents are identical with and without the rule.

## 6. Versioning and apply

`validator-v12` → **`validator-v13`**: every `llm` and `jsonld` Menu page becomes due for
re-interpretation from saved answers (no model call), about an hour on the laptop
(validator-v12's apply: 3,028 pages in 52 minutes). The PDF branch changes nothing on existing
data until discovery runs: the 164 `menu_pdf_only` venues are re-tried on their 20-day cooldown,
and any saved menu URL that serves a PDF is read on its next fetch.
