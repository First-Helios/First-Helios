# validator-v11: a numeric table's cells are no prices (P8-21, ADR-0013 Amendment 35)

Session P8-21, 2026-11-01, after the owner's answers on Amendment 35 (all as recommended: measure
the validator's numeric-row rule, build it as `validator-v11` if it loses no recall on the
labelled sets, then remove the false rows through the rule).

Scope:
- **Code:** the shared price reader (`packages/helios_parsing/prices.py`) and the validator's
  version. No model, prompt, chunker, schema or `infra/` change.
- **No writes to `helios_laptop` or the Pi.** Gold was measured read-only, and the apply was
  rehearsed on a scratch copy restored from the latest laptop dump (`helios_p821v11_test`).
- Scripts and outputs stay untracked in `var/p8-21/v11/` (worktree `p8-21-validator-v11`).

## Result

- **The rule:** a block that prints only an unmarked number is a numeric table's cell when it
  sits in a table row (at least 6 such blocks after one label block), in a sequence of at least
  3 consecutive rows. Mark cells ("X", "✓", "-") and blank blocks are skipped; a table split by
  section headings keeps its column count, so a one-row section of the same width is a row too.
  A cell gives no bare price reading and doesn't vote in `bare_page`. Money (a mark or two
  decimals) is read as before.
- **Labelled sets: no change at all.** Of 2,504 labelled or published page texts, the reader's
  tokens differ on exactly one: the P.F. Chang's nutrition table in the precision draw (labelled
  `not_menu`). The dev set's 1,685 measured pages (dev + corpus) have identical verdicts; the
  §8 harness report on the P8-21 held-out is identical (recall 0.895, accuracy 0.995, gold-row
  FR 0.072, usable 0.772); the invariance suite on 334 scorable dev pages has 0 differences.
- **Gold:** of the 2,039 stored texts behind `helios_laptop`'s Menu pages, the reader changes
  exactly **two**, both nutrition tables: P.F. Chang's "Menu Nutritionals To Go" (4 records) and
  Brio's "Menu Nutritionals" (1 record). These are the 5 records Amendment 35 found, with **1,354
  priced rows, all false**. Under `validator-v11` none of their rows keeps a price.

## 1. Mechanism (Amendment 35)

On a page that prints its prices bare (`bare_page`, v6), every nutrition cell is its own block
("2", "330", "120", …), and `_tokens` read each block's last integer as a bare price. The model
named the dish and took one of its row's numbers; the validator found that number printed right
after the name and accepted it: Chang's Chicken Lettuce Wraps at $330.00 (330 calories), Kids
Honey Chicken at $420.00, Mandarin Crunch Salad For One at $1.00 (servings).

## 2. Choosing the rule (`var/p8-21/v11/v11_runs.py`, `runs.txt`)

A first rule, "a bare number inside a run of K or more number-only blocks", fails on the dev set:
`dev:cfb443e9b654` prints its prices as a column after each section's dishes (runs of 6 to 19
bare prices, every one a gold price). Number-only runs on the labelled pages:

| Run length | Runs | Bare tokens | Of which a gold amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4,004 | 2,879 | 1,382 |
| 2 | 77 | 36 | 6 |
| 4–5 | 18 | 14 | 0 |
| 6–19 | 18 | 199 | 102 (all on `cfb443e9b654`'s price columns) |
| 31, 88 | 4 | 93 | 0 (date pickers, pagers) |

What separates a nutrition table from a price column is the row: a table repeats "one label
block, then the same cells", while a price column follows a whole section of names and
descriptions. So the rule needs repeated labelled rows, not a long run:
- **6 cells per row, at least.** Glass / half / bottle and size tables print 2–4 price columns;
  the nutrition rows print 10–12.
- **3 consecutive rows, at least**, one label between rows. Two rows could be a one-dish detail
  card; the tables in Gold have 50–200 rows.
- **Mark cells are skipped.** Brio's rows end with allergen marks ("X" ×0–7) before the next
  dish's name; without the skip no row followed another.
- **The table's width carries across section headings.** P.F. Chang's "Market Sides" section
  has one row under its own header row; it is read as a row because it has the table's 12 cells.
  With the width rule, 6 bare tokens are left on the 3,658-block page (amounts 0 and 1, below
  the validator's minimum amount), down from 2,790.

## 6. Versioning

- `validator-v10` → **`validator-v11`**. The reader is shared by the validator, stitch, the
  output repairs, [5b]–[5d] and [5c]'s DOM sections, and Amendment 15 recorded the last reader
  change (v7's bare one-decimal) under the validator's version alone. This follows it:
  `repairs-v9` and `resolve-v6` keep their names, because their own rules are unchanged and
  their input changes only where the reader's does (the two nutrition texts here).
- Every `llm` and `jsonld` Menu page's method version names the validator, so all of them become
  due for re-interpretation: `…;repairs-v9;validator-v11;resolve-v6;classifier-v2+v3` (127
  characters) and `jsonld-v1;validator-v11;segment-v2;…`. Saved answers are reused; no model
  call.
- The page classifier's frozen v4 reader (`price_tokens_v4`) is unchanged.
