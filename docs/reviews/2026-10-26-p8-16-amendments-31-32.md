# Amendments 31 and 32 proposed: scan continuation and shared section prices (P8-16)

Session P8-16, 2026-10-26. This record covers the prototypes and measurements behind two
proposals. Nothing in either is built, and both wait for the owner:
- [Amendment 31](../adr/0013-phase5-menu-pipeline.md#amendment-31-2026-10-26-proposed-the-validators-price-scan-continues-past-the-dishs-own-headings),
  the P8-x gap audit's F1;
- [Amendment 32](../adr/0013-phase5-menu-pipeline.md#amendment-32-2026-10-26-proposed-a-shared-section-price-as-a-5b-style-resolver),
  its F3, under the owner's Q2 limits.

The ADR text holds the measured tables and the owner questions. This record holds the method,
the per-page evidence and the reproduction.

Scope:
- **Dev numbers only.** The 793 gap-audit pages with labels-v10
  ([record](2026-10-26-p8-16-labels-v10.md), PR #129), and P8-11's 863-text corpus. None of it
  is a §8 result.
- **Code.** Both prototypes sit on `validator-v9` (Amendment 30's build, draft PR #128), as
  the task set them. They are patched in where `resolve.judge` calls the validator and [5b],
  so [5d], the re-judge, duplicate removal, the gold-row FR and corruption catch all see them.
  The Amendment 32 fill pass also wraps `page_template.llm_stream`.
- **Data.** There were no fetches, no model calls, no database access, and no laptop or Pi
  writes. No model, prompt or chunker change.

## Result

- **Amendment 31 (F1, heading part)** is worth building. On dev:
  - usable prices 0.8143 → 0.8212 (+0.0069 [+0.0026, +0.0126]);
  - gold-row FR 0.0958 → 0.0878;
  - catch flat at 0.9950;
  - price accuracy 0.9781 → 0.9787;
  - every Amendment 29 stage bar passes;
  - invariance flips fall in total (6,658 → 6,639).

  Two caveats:
  - one page adds two invariance pairs (variant in the name);
  - 13 gold items are lost through [5d]'s gate on three pages.

  F1's row part has no effect and is dropped.
- **Amendment 32 (F3, under Q2's limits)** reaches almost nothing.
  - Its first revision delivered +25 gold prices at the cost of 13 more wrong-price accepts.
    The guards that remove the wrong ones remove the gain: r3 fills 2 of 676 eligible rows.
  - The binding limit is Q2's own: the price is not on the heading or the line right under it
    (320 rows).
  - Finding sections by the model's section names breaks invariance.
  - Recommendation: do not build now. Moving a limit is an owner fork.

## 1. Amendment 31 (F1)

**Prototype.** `var/p8-16/proto/make_f1.py` writes `validator_f1.py` from `validator-v9` plus
the edits marked "(F1)":
- `_price_run` takes a `passes(j)` predicate, consulted only before a run starts;
- for a stop block, the row part, which is dropped;
- for a price-less heading, the heading part: the row's own name words (item codes aside), an
  item code alone, or a short line in the other script (CJK against Latin).

Sanity cases (`proto/sanity_f1.py`):
- a bilingual repeated heading and a "C2" code heading now ground;
- a section heading ("Drinks") still stops the scan;
- a description row was already passed by v6's `_NOT_ITEM`.

**Variants measured.** `f1` (both parts), `f1h` (heading part) and `f1r` (row part):
- `f1h` equals `f1` on every dev page;
- `f1r` changes no dev page and one corpus text.

Per-page gains and losses are in `var/p8-16/measure/am31-pages.txt`.

| Page | Gold prices gained / lost | Gold-row FR | What prints between name and price |
|---|---|---|---|
| 83b9a566697a | +59 / −8 | 122 → 10 | `li` 中文, `h3` name, `h3` 中文 |
| 05273895b4e2 | +23 | 88 → 54 | a card's item code ("C2") |

- **dc55a13f2101's losses** (`pagediff.py`): the model emits "腰果鸡虾 9.95" as a row of its own
  beside "Cashew Chicken and Shrimp 9.95". With F1, the Chinese row's scan passes the English
  `h3`, which is in the other script, and binds b0762 first. The English row is then
  `price_bound_to_other_item`.
- **v9's citations were wrong here.** v9 had accepted many of this page's English rows on the
  price printed just above the name, which is the previous dish's (b0509 over b0510). F1 cites
  the dish's own price below its record (b0515).
- **The lost gold items** (`lost_items.py`, `probe_tpl.py`): on held:c1ebd4d32417 the [5d] gate
  has 48 seeds and agreement 0.67 under F1, under the 0.8 gate. So [5d]'s ten additions there
  go: rows like "Duck Fried Rice 3", bare integers.

## 2. Amendment 32 (F3)

**Prototype.** `var/p8-16/proto/shared32.py`, as described in the ADR (accept pass in
`resolve.resolve`, fill pass after `llm_stream`). Revisions:
- **r1:** Q2's limits as stated, plus the model's section name as a heading;
- **r2:** marked prices only; dish headings head no section; a dish printed elsewhere with its
  own price is skipped; package, combo and per-person lines are menu-level;
- **r3:** a dish's own heading ends no section.

`s32h` is r1 without the model-section headings.

**Every `resolved_section_price` row, scored against gold** (`fills32-r1.txt`,
`fills32-r2.txt`, `fills32.txt`):

| Rows | r1 | r2 | r3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Right (the gold item's price) | 25 | 5 | 1 |
| False fill, scored: the dish prints its own price elsewhere | 13 | 1 | 0 |
| On items no label covers (unscored) | 91 | 26 | 32 |

What the unscored rows are, read one by one against the owner's kinds:
- **r1**, false fills among them:
  - "Pick 2 Lunch – $13.95" filled over 15 dishes that print their own prices elsewhere;
  - "Premium Package – $19/person/hour" over 6 spirits;
  - a date read as a price ("Available September 22", 3 dishes) and "Choice of 2" (2);
  - 83b9a566697a's dish headings taken as sections, 4 fills and its Chinese twin rows'
    accepts (a line outside the dish's section).
- **r2:**
  - "Big Yummm Deal" $16.99 over 2 burgers (a deal price);
  - a description row accepted at its dish's price;
