# validator-v12: a number after a calorie marker is no price (P8-22, ADR-0013 Amendment 36)

Session P8-22, 2026-11-18, under the owner's delegation (2026-11-17: "I trust you to make the
correct decision on the questions"). Found while measuring PDF menus for ADR-0029: Torchy's
gluten-conscious PDF prints "CAL 643" and today's reader takes 643 as a bare price. A check of
`helios_laptop`'s Gold found the same on HTML pages.

Scope:
- **Code:** the shared price reader (`packages/helios_parsing/prices.py`) and the validator's
  version. No model, prompt, chunker, schema or `infra/` change; the page classifier's frozen v4
  reader and the chunker are unchanged.
- **No writes to `helios_laptop` or the Pi.** Gold was measured read-only from the stored texts
  and saved answers. Scripts and outputs stay untracked in the session scratchpad.

## Result

- **The rule:** a bare reading (an unmarked integer or, on a bare page, a one-decimal amount) is
  not taken when a calorie marker comes right before it in its block: "cal 320",
  "Calories: 180", "kcal 450", "Cals: 340", and a range's last number ("cal 400-800",
  "calories: 610 – 920"). Such a number doesn't vote in `bare_page` either. Marked money reads as
  before ("Calories 320 $12.50" still reads $12.50).
- **Gold: 143 false rows, all of them.** Of the 2,039 stored texts behind `helios_laptop`'s Menu
  pages, the reader changes 10; re-interpreted from saved answers, two lose accepted prices, and
  everything they lose is a calorie count: Pokeworks (27 accepted rows → 0, e.g. "California Poke
  Bomb $320") and Quiznos (8 → 0, "Chipotle Chicken Zlider $180"). Boston Market and Saxbys print
  calories next to real prices; every accepted price on them is kept.
  Expected `current_menu` after the apply: **100,448 → 100,305**.
- **Labelled sets: no recall or accuracy loss.** 11 of 2,504 labelled or published texts change,
  every lost token a calorie count; the P8-21 held-out's §8 report and section report are
  identical; the verdict measurement is in section 3.

## 1. Mechanism

`_tokens` reads a block's last integer as a bare price when nothing but punctuation follows it.
A chain's menu often prints calories under each item's name and no price at all (Pokeworks:
"SPICY AHI" / "cal 400-800" / description; Quiznos: "calories: 180"). The model named the item and
copied the calorie number as its price; the validator found that number printed right after the
name and accepted it.

Gold on `helios_laptop` (read-only, every priced row's price Evidence against its block): **143
rows** carry a calorie count as their price, on 6 subjects and 2 page texts (Pokeworks' shared
menu page on 5 venues: 135 rows; Quiznos: 8).

## 5. Versioning

`validator-v11` → **`validator-v12`**, as Amendments 15 and 35 did for a reader change: the
reader is shared by the validator, stitch, the output repairs, [5b]–[5d] and [5c]'s DOM sections,
whose own versions (`repairs-v9`, `resolve-v6`) are unchanged. Every `llm` and `jsonld` Menu page
becomes due for re-interpretation from saved answers (no model call), as with `validator-v11`
(about 3,000 pages, about 2 hours on the laptop). The frozen `price_tokens_v4` (classifier-v2,
chunk-v2.3) is unchanged.

## 6. Not covered

- A calorie marker in another block ("Calories" as its own block above "320") is not covered; no
  Gold row was found in that shape.
- Calories printed after the number ("320 cal") never read as a price: a bare reading needs the
  number to end its block.
